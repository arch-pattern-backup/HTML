RMS amplitude (raw loudness)
Instantaneous volume, sampled continuously. Expect this to be spiky.
Smoothed loudness
RMS with a slow exponential moving average — the "drift" version.
Rolling variance
How much loudness is fluctuating in a recent window. Lower = more settled / regular.
Candidate coupling signal
1 minus normalized variance, itself slowly smoothed — a possible mapping toward "sympathetic accord" as variance drops.

This page only visualizes signals — it does not drive the pendulum. Once a candidate signal looks like it tracks "settling" on real recordings, that formula can be carried over to the pendulum page's coupling parameter.
