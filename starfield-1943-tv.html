A long time ago,
in a land far, far away…

STARFIELD
1943

In the Punjab, in the year nineteen forty-three, a life began that would require a specific kind of resilience.

You have carried that resilience across oceans, across decades, across every hard time that asked something of you.

You are turning eighty-three.

MEHR
Not earned. Not asked for.
The unearned kindness of the Creator —
a Grace-filled Hand
resting on the head of someone
navigating a hard time.
From its Persian root, Mihr —
the sun. Friendship. Warmth.
A light that dissolves the overwhelmed feeling
and replaces it with clarity.
You gave that word to your son.
He is holding it.

You are navigating your health with clarity and dignity — not just choosing a path for your recovery, but choosing how to live with intention.

That is a lesson worth crossing oceans for.

I cannot be there to walk with you this weekend. But I am standing under the same canopy you wished for me.

Between Seattle, Birmingham, and the village where you were born, we have managed to give you a forty-eight-hour birthday.

Every star in this field
is one day of your life.

29,389 DAYS
EIGHTY-THREE YEARS • EVERY ONE OF THEM YOURS
Happy Birthday, Dad.
I love you.
— Ran
