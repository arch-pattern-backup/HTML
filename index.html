📺 HOW TO VIEW THIS ON YOUR TV
A
Chromecast / Google TV
Open this page in Chrome on your phone or laptop. Tap the three-dot menu ⋮ and choose Cast… Select your TV. The page appears on the big screen. EASIEST
B
Android phone → Smart TV
Swipe down on your phone, tap Smart View (Samsung) or Screen Cast (other Android). Choose your TV. Your phone screen mirrors to the TV — open Dad Mode for the largest text.
C
iPhone / iPad → Apple TV or AirPlay TV
Swipe down from top-right corner, tap Screen Mirror, choose your Apple TV or AirPlay-compatible TV.
D
Laptop with HDMI cable
Plug an HDMI cable from your laptop to the TV. Switch the TV input to the HDMI port. Open this page in full-screen mode (F11 on Windows, Ctrl+Cmd+F on Mac).
★
Tip: Use Dad Mode when viewing on TV — the large text and year counter read clearly from across the room. Turn on Safe Mode to remove all flashing effects.
